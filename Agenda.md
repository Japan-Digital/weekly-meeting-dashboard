## Ground rules
- Whenever possible, attend in person.
- No laptops during the meeting unless you are presenting.
- Come prepared, having completed your weekly activities and updates before the meeting.

## Agenda

### Purposeful Moment
Each week, one team member will share something valuable with the group, such as an idea, innovation, customer insight, lesson learned, or industry update. No formal presentation is required.

### Utilisation Forecast Update (5 minutes per person)
Brief updates from everyone, including key priorities, blockers, risks, and any support needed from the team.

### Commission Excellence Audit
Random audit check. The audit check point must be based on our Service Delivery Excellence model built by (Naoki, Matt and Andrew)

### We WHEEL Together
The roulette wheel that picks a team member and a question.
